Our Vision for Texas

To tackle the challenges before us -- millions of us unable to see a doctor, an economy rigged to favor those at the top, and climate change driving a global crisis -- we need big, transformational change.

This is a campaign of, by, and for the people of District 25. It is a campaign for Medicare for All, a Green New Deal, a pathway to citizenship for the undocumented, an end to mass incarceration, and for a more just and equitable economy.

Julie's running a 100% PAC-free campaign. She doesn't take a single dime from any PAC. This is a campaign for the people of Texas -- the thousands of Texans from Wimberley to east Austin to Burleson and everywhere in between -- that Julie has met with and listened to while traveling hundreds of miles across this district to hear their stories and fight with them for real change.
