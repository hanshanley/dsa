VENISE IS THE STRONG DEMOCRAT WE NEED.

Venise knows the issues that matter to Nevada families. She won’t be pushed around on the issues that matter most to Nevadans. She wants to continue the work in Carson City for AD 10.

  • Address the affordability crisis head-on with legislation that helps Nevadans.

  • Lower housing costs by taking on greedy landlords to make it more affordable for Nevadans to rent or own a home.

  • Take on Big Pharma companies to lower the cost of prescription drugs and limit out-of-pocket costs for Nevadans.

  • Protect Choice and reproductive healthcare from MAGA extremists.

  • Pass a law that protects IVF so families who want to have children can make that choice.

  • Invest in our teachers, kids, and schools, including increasing pay for teachers and education support staff.

  • Provide funding so our teachers have school supplies.

  • Work to reduce class sizes.

  • Fight for free summer school and free school lunches.
