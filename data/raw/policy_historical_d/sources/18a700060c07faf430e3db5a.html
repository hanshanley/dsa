Looking for an endorsement?

 

Our endorsement process starts with filling out our official endorsement survey, to give us an indication of a candidate's background and platform. We then aim to schedule an interview with our Electoral Committee so we can have an in depth conversation with the candidate. The Electoral Committee votes on whether to refer an endorsement forward. If an endorsement passes the Electoral Committee, it comes to the next general meeting for approval by membership.
