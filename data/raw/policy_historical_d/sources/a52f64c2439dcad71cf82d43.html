Fighting for the
St. Louis We Deserve

I’m running for Congress to fight for a healthier, safer, and more affordable St. Louis for all of us. Sign up to get updates from our movement.

Here in St. Louis, the cost of daily necessities keeps going up. We’ve watched our grocery prices, rent, insurance, and utility bills climb while wages stay flat. Stable jobs are vanishing, our fundamental freedoms are under attack, and our families and neighbors are struggling while the rich get richer. 

The people of St. Louis feel the impact of failed leadership every single day, but it doesn’t have to be this way. We deserve a representative who shows up when it’s hardest, fights for us, and isn't afraid to stand up for what's right. That’s who I’ve always been: ten toes down for St. Louis.

I’m Cori Bush, a wife, mother, pastor, and a candidate to represent St. Louis in Congress.

I’m running to return as the Congresswoman for Missouri’s 1st District. In Congress, I'll continue my fight on behalf of St. Louis by focusing on securing historic funding for our community, lowering costs, and building an economy that works for all of us.

This campaign is about building a St. Louis where everyday people can thrive, not just survive. Together, we’re building a movement to take on corporate greed.

They’ll try to stop our grassroots campaign, just like they did in the last election. St. Louis is built for this fight, and so am I.
