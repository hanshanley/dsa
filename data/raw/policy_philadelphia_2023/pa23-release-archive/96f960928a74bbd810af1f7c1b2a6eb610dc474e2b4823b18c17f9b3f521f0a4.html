Check out the Questionnaire responses from City Council Candidates!

Reclaim Philadelphia asked candidates for City Council to answer questions about city policy and their worldview. Read their answers below and join us at our citywide meeting February 4th from 3pm - 4:30pm to hear from the some* of the candidates below as our member endorsement vote kicks off!

RSVP for our Citywide Meeting here!

Membership voting will take place through an online ballot starting February 4th. Only Reclaim members can vote in our endorsement process. Become a member by making a monthly dues payment of any amount here.
