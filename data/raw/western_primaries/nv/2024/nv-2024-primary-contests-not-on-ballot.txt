Democratic Party
Partisan Contests with No Democratic Party Candidates in the Primary
Election (in Ballot Order): The partisan contests below will not appear on
Democratic Party ballots in the Primary Election because they had only one
or no Democratic Party candidates. All of these contests will appear in the
2024 General Election. State Senate District 19 and State Assembly Districts
19, 22, and 23 had no Democratic Party candidates, so those contests will also
have no Democratic Party candidates in the General Election. All the other
contests had only one Democratic Party candidate and that candidate will
appear in the General Election.

Republican Party

Election (in Ballot Order): The partisan contests below will not appear on

All Other Candidates for Partisan Contests
Qualified candidates for partisan contests whose party designation is other
than Democratic or Republican will appear only on the General Election
ballot, not the Primary Election ballot, per NRS 293.257.
