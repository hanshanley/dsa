Assistant Minority Leader Senator Jeff Hayden Urges Rent and Mortgage Moratorium in Minnesota

  • by Senator Jeff Hayden
  • April 17, 2020April 22, 2020

SAINT PAUL, MN— The State of Minnesota has taken strong steps to ensure the health, safety, and economic security of Minnesotans are protected amid COVID-19. One being the suspension of evictions and foreclosures through an executive order issued by Governor Walz on March 23. However, with more Minnesotans facing unemployment and growing rent and mortgage debts, there is an increasing need to establish a rent and mortgage moratorium.

“Everyone Minnesotan deserves to have a safe, reliable place to call home. Right now, communities across the state are facing loss of income and increasingly exacerbated economic hardships during this public health emergency, while their rent and mortgage obligations continue to pile up,” said Assistant Minority Leader Senator Jeff Hayden. “Without the suspension of these payments we will see devastating consequences both during this public health emergency and after. Our priority should be to provide the immediate relief Minnesotans so greatly need in a timely manner, and that includes a moratorium on rent and mortgage payments.”

Executive Order 20-14 suspends evictions, termination of leases, and foreclosures in order to allow Minnesota families to remain sheltered during the peacetime emergency but does not relieve a tenant’s obligation to pay rent.

There is a bill in the House of Representatives, H.F. 4541, on eviction and mortgage foreclosure protection and emergency housing assistance during a public health emergency and can be found, here.

Senator Jeff Hayden
Senate District 62
Jeff Hayden represents District 62, which includes portions of south Minneapolis in Hennepin County in the Twin Cities metropolitan area.
