    RON'S MESSAGE

    I continue to remain humbled by the opportunity to serve the residents of the 23rd District.

    ​

    Our unique and diverse district continues to remain strong and vibrant and attract residents from all over the state. Our district is home to the County seat of government (Town of Upper Marlboro), the largest municipality in the County (City of Bowie), as well as Mitchellville, Beltsville, South Laurel and Cheltenham.  I am also proud that we have Maryland's oldest HBCU, Bowie State University in our district.​​
