Strengthening Our Schools

Raaheela believes strong public schools are the foundation of strong communities. As a former Prince George’s County School Board member, she fought to advance academic achievement and deliver real results for students and families. She’s ready to continue that work at the state level.

She supports:

Prince George’s County Public Schools
  • Fully funding our public schools so every student has the resources they need to succeed
  • Modernizing and maintaining safe, updated school buildings
  • Paying and retaining high-quality teachers and staff
  • Free early childhood education opportunities
  • Dismantling the school-to-prison pipeline through restorative and supportive practices
  • Promoting culturally responsive curriculum that reflects our diverse community
  • Increasing academic, mental health, and student support services
  • Ensure public education dollars stay in public schools
Bowie State University
  • Fully fund Bowie State University, our one HBCU in District 23
  • Keep college affordable for working families and first-generation students
  • Strengthen workforce development and research partnerships
  • Support campus improvements and student services

Strong county public schools and a strong Bowie State University mean more opportunity, stronger families, and a brighter future for District 23.

Dignity for Immigrant Families

Raaheela is the daughter of immigrants from Pakistan and India and grew up in a multigenerational household in Bowie. Her home was a place of grounding for relatives and friends who arrived as immigrants, refugees, and asylees.

She firmly opposes abusive and aggressive ICE enforcement practices that tear families apart and destabilize communities. She supports ending all voluntary local cooperation with federal immigration raids, banning 287(g) agreements statewide, prohibiting ICE access to schools, courthouses, and healthcare facilities, and strengthening data privacy protections to ensure Maryland remains a safe and welcoming state.

She also supports:

  • A path to citizenship for all U.S. immigrants
  • Legal and workplace protections for immigrant workers against wage theft, labor exploitation, etc.
  • Access to healthcare, higher education, and professional licensing regardless of status
  • A Maryland Dream Act expansion to include adult learners and workforce training programs
  • A right-to-counsel guarantee for immigrants facing deportation proceedings
  • Strong tenant protections and anti-discrimination enforcement for immigrant families

Immigrants strengthen our communities — and deserve dignity and opportunity.

Disrupting the Carceral System

Maryland has made progress, but our justice system is still deeply flawed. We struggle with racial inequity, accountability gaps, underinvestment in community services, and barriers to second chances. Raaheela believes public safety starts with strong communities — not mass incarceration.

She supports:

  • Major investments in mental health care and community-based services
  • A full ban on no-knock warrants
  • Ending qualified immunity so law enforcement can be held accountable
  • Strong community oversight of the justice system
  • Ending racial discrimination in arrests and sentencing
  • Fair labor standards in prisons
  • Guaranteeing a clear legal pathway for people convicted using discredited forensic evidence to return to court and pursue justice

Justice must mean fairness, accountability, and second chances — not punishment without reform.

Expanding Voter Access & Participation

Raaheela believes democracy should work for everyone — not just the wealthy and well-connected. For many years, she has worked professionally in democracy reform and civic engagement, and as a school board member, she pushed for transparency and accountability in government decision-making.

She supports:

  • Permanent universal vote-by-mail so every eligible voter can easily cast a ballot
  • Public financing of elections to reduce the influence of big money in politics
  • Special elections to fill vacancies, instead of insider political appointments
  • Universal voting, so every eligible resident has a voice in our elections

Raaheela believes voting should be simple, accessible, and protected because democracy is strongest when everyone can participate.

Healthcare For All

Raaheela knows firsthand what it means to struggle with medical bills. As a child dealing with multiple health issues, her family faced repeated hospital visits and overwhelming costs. She believes healthcare is a human right, not a luxury.

She supports:

  • A universal, single-payer healthcare system
  • Minimal to no out-of-pocket costs for prescriptions and medical devices
  • Expanded mental health services
  • Lower prescription drug prices
  • Protecting coverage for pre-existing conditions
  • Reproductive justice and bodily autonomy

No one should go into debt just to stay alive or care for their family.

Protecting Our Planet

We only have one planet — and we must protect it for future generations. As a former National Leadership Council member for Elected Officials to Protect America, Raaheela is committed to bold climate action in Maryland.

She supports:

  • 100% clean energy, water and air
  • Zero-emissions transportation
  • Eliminating landfill and food waste
  • No artificial intelligence data centers in District 23 that strain energy and water resources
  • Environmental justice policies that protect communities of color from pollution

Climate change is not a future problem — it’s happening now. Maryland must lead, and execute on its promises to our people.

Upholding Workers’ Rights

Raaheela has organized to unionize in her own previous workplace and understands how hard people work just to get by. She comes from a union family — the granddaughter of a union hotel housekeeper and the daughter of a union healthcare worker — and knows the power of collective bargaining.

She supports:

  • A true living wage
  • Paid family and medical leave
  • A fair and predictable workweek

Every worker deserves dignity, stability, and fair pay.

Keeping Corporations Accountable

With degrees in finance and economics, and experience as a federal financial consultant, Raaheela understands how corporate systems are often built to benefit the wealthy at everyone else’s expense.

She supports:

  • Closing corporate tax loopholes
  • Ensuring corporations pay their fair share
  • Cracking down on predatory lending practices
  • Capping excessive interest rates and hidden fees
  • Protecting consumers from exploitative financial products

Our economy should work for working families — not just shareholders.

Expanding Affordable Housing

Raaheela grew up in District 23 and still lives in one of its few affordable neighborhoods. She understands how expensive it has become to stay in the community you love. She believes housing is a human right.

She supports:

  • Collective living, like housing cooperatives (co-ops) and social housing
  • Strong tenant protections, including just-cause eviction
  • Expanding the Renter’s Rights and Stabilization Act
  • Incentives to build truly affordable housing, not just luxury development

No one should have to choose between paying rent and paying for groceries.

Standing Up for Women

Raaheela is a strong supporter of women’s rights and reproductive justice. She will work to protect women, and ensure that they can make their own healthcare and lifestyle decisions.

She supports:

  • Free childcare for ages 0–3
  • Pay equity to address the gender pay gap
  • Strengthening maternal health systems
  • Addressing the Black maternal mortality crisis
  • Workplace protections against sexual harassment

Women’s freedom includes bodily autonomy, economic independence, and safety in every sphere of life.
