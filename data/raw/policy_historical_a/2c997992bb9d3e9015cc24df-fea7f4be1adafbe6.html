Issues

Healthcare
Our current healthcare system leaves thousands without coverage in our state alone. With the most recent attacks on the Affordable Care Act, that problem is set to worsen. We need a new system that prioritizes healthcare access of all residents above all else. That is why Kareem supports the push to pass a single-payer system right here in Pennsylvania that will save lives while creating new jobs in the region.

 

As we seek to improve our current healthcare policy, particular attention must be given to mental health. Traditionally, mental health services are not prioritized as other medical services are. With a severe lack of providers in our district and beyond, our district’s mental and physical health has suffered. This is reflected in the growing opioid crisis, where our current providers do not have the resources necessary to provide care. Kareem believes we must guarantee proper funding for mental and behavioral health care services to meet the needs of the community.

 

Environment
Air and water pollution are problems throughout the state, with Allegheny County being one of the worst at maintaining healthy standards for its residents. Pennsylvania paid over a billion in corporate tax incentives to encourage the building of a cracker plant in neighboring Beaver County with the promise of 600 permanent jobs, but these plants put the air quality of the entire region at risk for relatively little gain. With several more scheduled for future construction, now is the time to end construction of new cracker plants.

 

Fracking leaks gas and particulates into the air and surrounding groundwater, which puts our loved ones and children at risk of developing a myriad of health problems. We must heavily regulate existing worksites to mitigate these risks and seriously reconsider future fracking sites. In addition to the environmental considerations, our state should be compensated for its resources. Kareem will work to institute a severance tax to compensate for the natural gas extraction and an impact fee for the damage done to our infrastructure, which will help fund healthcare and education initiatives in the region.

 

Education
The American public education system is in crisis. After several rounds of defunding and misappropriation of funds, school conditions are going downhill nationwide. While Republicans believe that the best solution is cutting school budgets and turning your tax dollars over to unaccountable private bodies, Kareem believes that we can do better for our children. Our schools are the pipelines that lead our educated youth to stable, well-paying employment. Attacks on our educational system are direct attacks on the middle class upon which our communities are built.

 

Reproductive Rights
It is not enough to just defend reproductive rights and the "right to choose" especially in the face of coordinated attacks on women's health by Republicans. We must pass comprehensive legislation ensuring women's complete bodily autonomy and continued access to necessary services like affordable contraception, abortion services, prenatal/pregnancy care, and family planning.

 

Justice Reform
Change in our justice system is necessary and long overdue. Battling the opioid epidemic through punitive laws and mass incarceration is both misguided and a failed experiment. Professional health resources must be made available to those struggling with substance use disorder. Kareem will work to redirect drug offenders away from incarceration and towards treatment.
