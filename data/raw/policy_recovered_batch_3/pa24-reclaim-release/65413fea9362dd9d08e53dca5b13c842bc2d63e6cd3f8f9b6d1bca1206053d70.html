2024 Candidate Questionnaire Responses

Reclaim Philadelphia asked candidates for State Representative, State Senate, State Treasurer, Attorney General and Auditor General to answer questions about state policy. Read their answers below and join us at our citywide meeting February 24th from 3pm - 4:30pm to hear from the candidates as our member endorsement vote kicks off!

RSVP here!

Membership voting will take place through an online ballot from February 24th through March 2nd. Only Reclaim members can vote in our endorsement process. Become a member by making a monthly dues payment of any amount here.
