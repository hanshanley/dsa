Explain, based on life experiences and accomplishments, why you believe you are best qualified to represent your district
I am very proud to serve in the United States Congress and believe I am uniquely qualified to represent our district, which spans parts of Queens, Nassau and Suffolk counties. This is considered a swing district that leans Democratic. I am an ardent supporter and advocate for our Democratic values, but understand that if we go too far left we risk losing this seat and losing seats like this will put our majority in the House in jeopardy. We must win more House seats and increase our representation in the Senate and ultimately elect a Democrat President in 2020.

If applicable, what legislation directly affecting the LGBT community have you introduced or co-sponsored? (indicate accordingly).

  • Wrote an op-ed in the NYT in 2009 supporting gay marriage.

  • Co-sponsored HR 5, Equality Act

  • Fought for funding for:
    • The State Department and USAID to preserve human rights of LGBTI persons and protect vulnerable individuals in the LGBTI community
    • The for-President’s Emergency Plan for AIDS Relief (PEPFAR) and the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria (Global Fund).

  • Sent a letter to acting Secretary of Labor Patrick Pizzella to withdraw a rule change to an Obama executive order that banned discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity.

  • Cosponsor of International Human Rights Defense Act, establishes a permanent Special Envoy within State Department for LGBTI people.

  • Cosponsor of H Res 124, expressing opposition to banning service in the Armed Forces by openly transgender individual.

  • Cosponsor of Every Child Deserves a Family Act, would prohibit a child welfare agency receiving federal financial assistance from discriminating against any potential foster or adoptive family on the basis of religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or marital status.
