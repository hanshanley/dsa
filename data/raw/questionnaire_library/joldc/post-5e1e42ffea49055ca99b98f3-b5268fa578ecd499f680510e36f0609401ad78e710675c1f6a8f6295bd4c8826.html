Explain, based on life experiences and accomplishments, why you believe you are best qualified to represent your district
For too long everyday working people have gone ignored across the country and district.  My family represents what it means to be ignored by representatives: a coal miner killed by corporate greed, a Japanese immigrant who faced racism and xenophobia, a mechanic who fell and became paralyzed.  How is it that the Americans with Disabilities Act is 29 years old, but in the largest city in the United States, the majority of our subway stations are not accessible? How is it that we are just now starting to prioritize the Green New Deal and saving our planet when we have known for so long that we are facing disastrous climate change? How is it that we still rely on such a broken, for-profit healthcare system?  Why are there still thousands of people sleeping on the streets in NY-12 alone? We need a people-powered advocate to represent NY-12 and stand up to big money in politics. I am a working resident of NY-12 with a family that has experienced the issues that many face across the country, and it is time that a grassroots organizer who has created real, grassroots change in her community becomes the vocal advocate of the district.

Describe any federal legislation and policy changes that you support in order to address the ongoing effects of slavery, racism, colonialism, and discrimination 
I support reparations and true criminal justice reform, including ending arrest quotas, ending cash bail, decriminalizing poverty, encouraging the development of positive relationships between police forces and the communities they serve, and ending for-profit prisons and mass incarceration which has historically impacted People of Color at a much higher rate.  I also support federal legislation requiring an outside special prosecutor for cases involving killings by police to eliminate conflicts of interest. I also am for pay transparency at employers with over 199 employees, which will combat the wage gap.

What legislation or other policy changes do you support in order to make college and graduate school affordable for poor, working-class, and middle-class Americans and to alleviate the crushing loan debt that many students and alumni are facing?
I support abolishing student debt, making higher education free, and also providing a credit to those who have paid off their student debts so that they can reinvest this money in their futures.
