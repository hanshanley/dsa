“I’m Julie–an immigrant, mom, and renter–and as Council Member, I’ve added over 32,000 new homes and secured $2 billion for schools, parks, public housing, transportation, and resiliency. I believe that from the moment you’re born until the day you retire, this country should have your back. I’m running for Congress to build a Lifetime of Care, policies that support us at every stage of life.”

Issues

  1. A Lifetime of Care
  2. Affordable housing
  3. Immigration

Public safety requires more than just law enforcement. We need to take a holistic approach to meet everyone’s basic needs, including greatly expanding mental health resources, creating job opportunities, funding after-school programming, and building affordable housing.

In four years, I have built and approved 32,000 new homes and ensured that over 12,000 of them are deeply affordable. In Congress, I will continue to protect tenant rights, support homeowners, revitalize public housing, and build even more affordable housing.

As a mom of two and provider for my aging parents, I feel the rising cost of living every day: rent, childcare, groceries – sometimes it feels expensive to breathe. That’s why we need a Lifetime of Care, not just so we can survive, but thrive.

We need universal healthcare for all. Nobody should go bankrupt because they got sick or skip a doctor’s visit because they can’t afford the copay. I will invest in public hospitals, hold insurance companies accountable, and continue fighting for healthcare for all.

My family and I came to New York when I was 8, and over half of my constituents share that experience. As Council Member, I welcomed over 15,000 asylum seekers, funded language coops, and more. In Congress I will abolish ICE, end family separation, and protect families like ours.

The root cause of homelessness is impossibly expensive housing. I built or approved 12,000 deeply affordable homes, including dedicated units for the formerly homeless and supportive housing with wraparound support. In Congress, I will expand these programs.support.

As the co-Chair of the Black, Latino, and Asian Caucus and a member of the Committee to Combat Hate, I am committed to eliminating the racial wealth gap and to stand up to anti-Black racism, anti-semitism, Islamophobia, anti-Asian hate, and xenophobia.

A product of New York public schools myself, I secured more than $100 million for education and created almost 6,000 new seats for children of all ages. We need to pay our teachers fairly, restore federal funding, and make after-school programs match real work hours.
