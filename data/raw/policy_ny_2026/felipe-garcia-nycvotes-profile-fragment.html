Issues

  2. Fund Mental Health Programs for All
  3. Banning Inside Trading for Congress

I support a practical approach to public safety, making sure law enforcement has the resources they need at every level while working closely with the community to prevent crime before it starts. Safer streets come from both strong support and real partnership.

By increasing investment in mixed-income housing, streamlining zoning, and protecting tenants, we can expand affordable housing in NYC. People deserve a safe, stable place to live, and I am committed to making sure working families can still afford to call this city home.

Make everyday life easier for working families through better pay, stable housing costs, and support for local businesses that keep prices fair. People should feel the difference in their paycheck and at the grocery store. Bottom line: life should not be this stressful to get by.

Everyone should be able to see a doctor without stressing about the bill. I want to expand coverage, lower prescription costs, and ensure mental health care is there when you need it. Healthcare shouldn’t feel out of reach; it should just be there for you.

I believe in fair and compassionate immigration policies. We should protect immigrant rights, simplify the path to citizenship, and keep families together. We also need clear, legal pathways to residency and a city where immigrants can build a life, contribute, and succeed.

I believe in addressing homelessness with compassion and a housing-first approach. I will support more supportive housing, expanded mental health services, and access to job training. Everyone deserves a stable place to live and a real path to rebuild their lives.

I am committed to addressing racial inequality by expanding fair access to education, healthcare, housing, and jobs. I will work to confront systemic bias and support policies that strengthen underserved communities. The goal is a more just and equal city and country.

I will raise high school graduation rates into the 90s by creating real incentives to finish school, including direct access to apprenticeships and job placement after graduation. Every student deserves a clear path to success. When students see a future, they stay in school.
