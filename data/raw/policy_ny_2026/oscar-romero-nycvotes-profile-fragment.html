“I’m Oscar J. Romero Jr., a Mexican-American, educator, and NYC public servant living in East Harlem. For 7 years, I worked on 75+ projects on mental health services, tenant rights, immigrant services and violence prevention. This campaign reflects the hopes of the 500k+ New Yorkers we served. I’m running for Congress to abolish ICE, protect civil rights, and end US forever wars. It’s time for leadership with the values and experience needed to achieve Medicare for All, tuition-free public college, and social housing.”

Issues

  1. Abolish ICE & End US Wars
  2. Free Higher-Ed & Medicare for All
  3. Fund NYCHA & Build Social Housing

We must go beyond punishment and policing towards restorative justice. We must create opportunities and equity to address the root causes of violence, while funding initiatives to dismantle the abuse of mass incarceration, segregation, police brutality, and surveillance.

Access to quality, affordable housing is fundamental to break with poverty cycles. I support fully funding NYCHA, expanding tenant protections, investing in community revitalization grants, making housing vouchers work, and providing funding for first-time home buyers.

The federal government is creating an affordability crisis in NYC fueled by Trump’s tariffs, his illegal war with Iran, and unchecked corporations. The 1% profit while we pay the bill. We must end senseless wars, build strategic trade agreements and expand antitrust laws.

The path is clear, we must reform the whole healthcare system and provide Universal Healthcare. We must implement Medicare for All, reduce the cost of prescription drugs, expand access to mental health services, and protect access to gender-affirming care.

I believe in creating an immigration system that treats people with dignity. In Congress, I will fight to abolish and prosecute ICE, end the criminalization of migration, end for profit detention, ensure due process, and create pathways to citizenship and work documentation.

Homelessness is the consequence of the failure of our government to provide housing, healthcare, and opportunity. Our unhoused neighbors deserve comprehensive policies that provide street-to-home case work, pathways for employment, and long term affordable housing.

Everything is segregated. 7/10 people in prison are Black & Brown. The Black community in NYC (22% of the population) owns only 3.5% of the businesses. PoC have the worst health and educational outcomes. We must fight for reparations, call out anti-blackness, and demand equity.

We must protect public education. Education should be about setting up our youth for success not about making money. We must increase funding to support mental health services, support culturally competent programming, and ensure equitably funded desegregated schools.
