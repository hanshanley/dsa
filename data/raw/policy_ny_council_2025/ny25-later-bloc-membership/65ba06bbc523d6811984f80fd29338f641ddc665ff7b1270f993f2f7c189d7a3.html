Chapters Prepare for a Busy Election Season in 2026

Adam Kaiser

The map of DSA endorsements below (under the “Upcoming Elections” heading) gives some sense of DSA’s ambitious electoral agenda in 2026: Democratic Left counts almost 90 endorsees, including more than a dozen congressional candidates. Chapters can submit “Chapter and Verse” items at the second form listed here.

ORGANIZING

Top Growing Chapters (January 2025)

Top Member Growth / New York City +5.4% (+711)

Top Percent Growth / Sioux Empire +60.4% (+29)

Numbers reflect members in good standing.

Chapter Membership Milestones (January)

More than 100 Members: Lawrence (Kan.), Pensacola (Fla.), Spokane (Wash.). 500 Members: Richmond (Va.), Sacramento (Calif.), Tacoma and Pierce County (Wash.). 1,000 Members: Greater Baltimore. 2,000 Members: Twin Cities (Minn.)

Ranking Changes in the Top 20 Chapters

San Francisco (84 new members) overtakes Detroit (69) to become the 14th largest chapter.

New Chapters and Organizing Committees (February)

Organizing Committees: Wooster (Ohio), Cadillac (Mich.), Maui (Hawaii)

Launch The Nutmeg Socialist / Connecticut DSA

Connecticut DSA published the first edition of its new chapter publication, The Nutmeg Socialist, in February.

Expands Territory, Changes Name / Piedmont Triad (NC)

Winston-Salem DSA will now be known as Piedmont Triad DSA and operate with an expanded jurisdiction including Guilford, Forsyth, Randolph, Rockingham, Davidson, Davie, Yadkin, Surrey, and Stokes countiesin North Carolina. The “Piedmont Triad” refers to three clustered cities in north-central North Carolina: Winston-Salem, Greensboro and High Point.

ELECTORAL

Moreno Wins in First Post-Mamdani Test / New York City

Astoria elected DSA- and Zohran Mamdani-endorsed Diana Moreno as the mayor’s successor in the state legislature in New York City DSA’s first electoral test after Mamdani’s election and a hopeful preview for the chapter’s ambitious slate of candidates for the state legislature and U.S. Congress on the ballot in June.

“I hope Kathy Hochul is paying attention. By electing Diana, New Yorkers are yet again demanding something that the governor desperately wants to ignore: we need to tax the rich,” said co-chair Gustavo Gordillo wrote in a statement after the election. “In the richest city in the world, it is obscene to cut funding from social programs so that billionaires can add another zero to their bank accounts, yet that’s exactly what Hochul wants. The governor needs to listen to the people and to common sense, not to her oligarch donors.”

Back Candidate for Mayor / Metro DC

Metro DC DSA announced its endorsement of Janeese Lewis George (DSA-Ward 4) for DC mayor on Feb. 6. Lewis George was endorsed by DSA in her 2024 campaign for City Council.

Celebrate Bush, Hartzell Filing / Missouri chapters

Members of Mid-Missouri, St. Louis and Kansas City DSA helped organize a joint rally at the state capitol to celebrate candidate filing for two endorsed congressional candidates: Cori Bush, running to return to Congress representing the St. Louis-based first district and Hartzell Gray, running to represent the fourth district, which includes parts of Kansas City. Columbia city councilmember Vera Elwood (DSA-Ward 2) emceed.

SOCIALISTS-IN-OFFICE

Add two to City Council Bloc / New York City

New York City DSA admitted Chi Ossé and Shahana Hanif  to its Socialists-in-Office bloc on New York City City Council, doubling the chapter’s presence in the chamber.

Leads Formation of Progressive Caucus / Atlanta

Georgia State Rep. Gabriel Sanchez (DSA-Smyrna) will co-chair a progressive caucus in the Georgia state legislature. The caucus was announced Feb. 20 and includes 13 representatives and senators.

“The Progressive Caucus is a positive force focused on solutions that put working people first. Together, we’re creating a vision of a Georgia where our government represents all of us, not just those at the top. That means working-class families who keep this state running can afford to live here, raise their children, and thrive in their communities,” Sanchez wrote in a statement announcing the caucus’s formation.

LABOR

Statement: Public Sector Right to Strike / Maryland chapters

“Despite serving on the front lines of caring for our communities day in and day out, teachers and other public workers in Maryland are denied organized labor’s most powerful tool- the strike. We, the Maryland chapters and branches of DSA, believe that organized labor is a primary vehicle for building working class power, fostering solidarity and challenging the capitalist system. Unions and the right to strike are essential not only for better working conditions but for democratizing the workplace itself and fomenting the class consciousness necessary for the social transformation and better tomorrow that we organize for.” (link)

AFFORDABILITY

Statement: Governor Hochul: Which side are you on? / New York City

“Mayor Mamdani’s preliminary budget makes it clear that Gov. Hochul and the State legislature have two options: Tax The Rich to make New York affordable, or declare war on the affordability agenda in order to shield the bank accounts of the billionaire Epstein class. If Hochul upholds the status quo and chooses the latter, small homeowners and tenants alike will feel the squeeze from Hochul’s Homeowner Tax while multimillionaires reap $12 billion dollars from federal tax cuts.” (link)

Amendment Strengthens Millionaire Tax / Seattle

Washington State Representative Shaun Scott (DSA-43) pushed a successful amendment to a proposed “millionaires tax” bill designed to compensate for federal cuts to social service funding. Unamended, the bill would have sunsetted a tax on big corporations early, costing the state an estimated $550 million.

Launch Public Power Pledge / Metro DC

We Power DC, a project of Metro DC DSA calling for a public power utility for the District of Columbia, has launched a new public power pledge for 2026.

HOUSING

Statement: Against Slashing Transfer Tax / San Francisco

“We know that giving tax breaks to speculators and billionaires doesn’t work to build housing. After San Francisco slashed affordable housing requirements in 2023, more new housing wasn’t built — in fact, 2024 saw the fewest housing permits filed in more than a decade… Rather than double down on failed neoliberal strategies, we should directly invest in housing San Franciscans of all incomes, using proven funding strategies like Prop I.” (link)

Green Social Housing Resolution Passes / Austin

A resolution advanced by city councilmember Mike Siegel (DSA-7) directing Austin’s city manager to explore green social housing as an affordable housing solution for the city was passed by Austin city council in February.

Campaign for Eviction Moratoriums / Twin Cities

With an immigration enforcement surge keeping people in the Twin Cities at home and away from work, Twin Cities DSA has joined organizations campaigning for a moratorium on evictions at the local and state level.

Protect Rent Stabilization / Metro DC

The Montgomery County branch is organizing to protect Takoma Park Maryland’s rent stabilization law, flyering and circulating a petition as the city government brings in a consultant that may recommend a loosening of tenant protections.

ECOLOGY

Launch Light Rail Campaign / Atlanta DSA

Members launched a campaign to expand Atlanta’s light rail system — to “Build Beltline Rail” — with canvases in February.

POLICING

INTERNATIONAL

Statement: On Iran Attacks / Denver

“The Denver Democratic Socialists of America (DDSA) echoes national DSA’s condemnation of the American-Israeli aggression against Iran. These unprovoked strikes have already killed at least 85 people at an elementary school for girls, the images of blood-stained backpacks mirroring the carnage and unrestrained slaughter of Gazan children we have all witnessed for the last two years. This is not coincidental – the brutality inflicted on Palestinians was never going to be limited to the land between the river and the sea, but was a step towards normalizing a more unmasked version of American imperialism around the world. From the unlawful abduction of Nicholas Maduro in Venezuela to the brutal blockade against the Cuban people, from Palestine to Iran, we are witnessing a dying empire lashing out in desperate attempts to reinforce its might around the world, for the benefit of a ruling class and no one else.” (link)
