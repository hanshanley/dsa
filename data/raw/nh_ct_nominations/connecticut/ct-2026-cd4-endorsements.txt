Who may use this form? Major party candidates who have been endorsed, major party candidates who have received 
at least 15% of the vote, or candidates who have been nominated by a minor party with statewide ballot access, as 
well as the party Chairperson, Presiding Officer, or Secretary.
  
How do I file? Forms may be submitted to the Secretary of the State in one of two ways: electronically using Docusign 
or on paper. Paper forms should be returned by mail or in person to the Secretary of the State,  
ATTN: LEAD at 165 Capitol Ave., Suite 1000, Hartford, CT 06115. The office is open Monday – Friday, from  
8:30 am to 4:00 pm. Candidate and party representative must complete the form for it to be considered filed. 
 
What is the filing deadline? The major party deadline to file is 4:00 pm, no later than 14 days after the meeting.  
(If this day falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or Legal Holiday, the deadline is the next business day at 4:00 pm).  
The deadline for minor parties is September 2, 2026, at 4:00 pm.
