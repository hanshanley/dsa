OUR PLATFORM

Aprile Hearn has been involved with community engagement and public service for over two decades in district 5. She lives, works, worships, and raised her family within the district. As a democratic socialist, Aprile is dedicated to fighting for the working class and the unseen in our community.

 

Together with the residents of district 5, Aprile will lead the community to reduce harm, improve the health of our neighborhoods, and elevate quality of life for working class people. Aprile has learned through personal experience what it takes to find solutions, navigate roadblocks, and get things done.

A VOTE FOR DEMOCRACY IN OUR LIVES​

  • Create an advisory council for district 5 so that the people can decide on discretionary spending

  • Public input on the city budget through town halls

  • End corporate giveaways to developers of our land and taxpayer dollars

  • Fully fund TARC through a ballot referendum for free buses and expanded services

A STRONGER LOUISVILLE

  • Support community centered-and-run programs that keep kids in school and off the streets and address the root cause of violence

  • Invest in public spaces and resources like parks, libraries, community healthcare and affordable housing that’s actually affordable

  • Stand up to Trump and Frankfort Republicans telling us how to run our city

  • Fight for solutions to homelessness that get people off the streets

A HEALTHIER WEST END

  • Bring LG&E into public ownership

  • Stop hyperscale data centers being built in our communities

  • End food deserts with city-run grocery stores for healthy food

  • Provide green spaces for horticultural projects 

  • Protect our children by reinstating protections against lead poisoning removed by Metro Council in 2025—a removal our current representative voted for
