Meet Ashleigh

Ashleigh Rogers is proud of her deep roots in hardworking Southwest Missouri communities. Raised in Dade County, she developed a strong sense of integrity, collaboration, and commitment to the common good.

After graduating from Kansas State University, Ashleigh moved to Houston, Texas, where she began her career in Interior Design. During her time there, she built lasting friendships and gained valuable perspectives that reinforced her belief in the shared values that unite people across the country.

She returned to Missouri 11 years ago and continued her work in Interior Design with an Architecture firm in Kansas City. In recent years, she transitioned into a leadership role as Americas Resource Manager, where she supports more than 600 employees nationwide by aligning individual strengths with career development opportunities.

Ashleigh lives near the Plaza in Kansas City with her partner and their two sons.
