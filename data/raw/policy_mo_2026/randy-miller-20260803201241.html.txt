For Real. Change.
My name is Randy Miller and I am running as a common sense candidate for the 4th congressional district in Missouri.
Learn More
Universal healthcare for all.
Expanding Whiteman AFB into the largest defense and technological hub of the midwest, to create quality jobs, manufacturing and infrastructure.
No property tax for disabled veterans, regardless of their disability rating.
More subsidies for farmers, and a focus on renewable energy.
Immediate moratorium for building data centers.  We need to stop this now.
Tax hike for anyone making 5 million or more per year.
Tax incentives for builders who build truly affordable housing.
Total support of the Keep the Pay Act, which exempts tax for couples filing jointly until they earn their first $75,000.
Dramatic increase for teacher salaries.
Complete restructuring of the Federal Communications Commission in support of our disappearing First Amendment rights.
