Pugsley Stands
He was fat and four-eyed with thick black glasses and a big nose. Girls petrified him. Bullies pulverized him. Then, a dream as a teen directed the destiny of his life. Once King of the Jungle Gym Bars in kindergarten and Knight of the Year in high school, Pugsley was duped by business partners, chased by the Ku Klux Klan, falsely arrested and jailed nine times, survived twenty-three car wrecks, and endured countless other calamities while fighting racism and injustice as an Army combat medic, missionary, father, teacher, coach and nurse. Often at odds with the establishment, Pugsley defied convention to emerge as a champion and embodiment of hope for the underserved and those pummeled by injustice.
Wayne Russell is a passionate author and social activist dedicated to illuminating the lives of those often overlooked. His work reflects a deep commitment to social justice that fosters understanding and inspires change. Wayne aims to empower readers to challenge societal norms and engage with their communities.
