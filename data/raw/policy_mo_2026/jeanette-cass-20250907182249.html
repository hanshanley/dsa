    Meet Jeanette

    Lifelong Missouri Farmer

    Jeanette Cass is a lifelong Missourian, a farmer, and a community advocate. She’s spent decades working the land, supporting her neighbors, and standing up for the values that keep rural Missouri strong. Now she’s ready to take that fight to Washington.

    Affordable cost of living

    Rent, groceries, prescription drugs—all sky high with inflation and price gouging. Enough is enough. It is long past time our congressperson brought home lower prices for our families, instead of DC political BS.

    Fund transportation

    We Missourians need to be connected to each other—within our cities, between our counties, and within our state! I will fight for federal dollars for public transportation, rural commuter rail routes, and equitable access for everyone across our state.

    Protect our family farms

    Corporations, foreign interests, and private equity are pushing us out of our family farms. Our government needs to support us farmers, not make our lives more difficult.

    Repair crumbling rural infrastructure

    For far too long Washington has left our communities behind. We need real infrastructure—good roads, strong bridges, and fast connection to the greater community.

    Ensure affordable healthcare

    Healthcare should not be a bank-breaking expense, going to the doctor should be affordable and easy. We need affordable healthcare and investments to stop our rural hospitals from closing.
