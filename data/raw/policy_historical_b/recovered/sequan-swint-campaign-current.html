Sequan Swint works with community organizers and local leaders to scale household and community-level improvements. Our work is based on the belief that local action, propelled at scale, is critical for reaching our district goals.

The Four Pillars

Our commitment to District 9's future

Public Health

Building Agri-Wellness Hubs and Med-Tail clinics to end the medical desert. Healthcare shouldn't require crossing county lines.

Infrastructure Accountability

Launching the District 9 Audit Squad to verify 311 repairs. Every pothole reported, every repair verified.

Worker Protections

Implementing "Local-First" mandates to keep our dollars in the district. Our workers deserve our contracts.

Education & Legacy

Protecting our seniors with tax relief and creating lifelong "Mastery Centers." Honoring those who built our community.
