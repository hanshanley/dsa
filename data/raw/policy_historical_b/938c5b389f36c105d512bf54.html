Emily's 2026 Platform

Protecting Our Climate & Lowering Your Bills

As your Assemblymember, I am committed to fighting for a green future for all of us. New York is on the front lines of climate change, and we must lead the nation in addressing it. Climate change is a present threat, and it’s costing you money.

Emily’s Solution: Leading on Green Energy

The All-Electric Buildings Act

Through widespread corporate opposition, I passed this bill in 2023 to ensure that new buildings would run on electricity, not fossil fuels & gas. Now the mission is implementing it! The Governor and pro-gas billionaires have delayed the requirements, and our planet can’t afford delays!

The SUNNY Act

This bill is modeled after European innovations, and would legalize the use of balcony-based personal solar panels for all New Yorkers–putting your energy into your own hands and lowering your bills for good!

Truly Affordable Housing

Empty promises are made every day about affordability in housing, especially in NYC. Truly affordable housing is nearly impossible to find, and getting rarer every day.

Emily’s Solution: Social Housing

The Social Housing Development Authority

The Social Housing Development Authority, aka House The Future, would build thousands of new units, set to only 25% of a tenant’s income. These units would be:

  1. Democratically controlled and tenant-organized
  2. Permanently rent controlled
  3. 100% union-built
  4. Completely reliant on clean, green energy

Not every housing bill changes the reality of housing production. This will.

Saving Our Small Businesses

Small businesses are at the heart of our communities and our city’s history, but more and more we see them pushed out of their leases and the city entirely. There is currently no guarantee that a commercial lease will be renewed, or that the rents won’t skyrocket.

Emily’s Solution: Commerical Rent Stabilization

The NYC Commercial Rent Stabilization Act

The NYC Commercial Rent Stabilization Act would finally create a Rent Guidelines Board for commercial leases, ensuring an independent ruling on how much rent could go up–if at all.

8,400 businesses shut down in NYC in a single quarter last year. 12% of our storefronts are vacant, including 1 in 5 in Manhattan. Let’s end that.

Making Our Streets Safer

For years and years, through countless preventable deaths and countless delays and canceled projects, I made it my mission to fix McGuinness Boulevard, one of the most dangerous streets in the city. It’s not an anomaly; our streets are designed for cars only and pedestrians and bikers suffer.

Emily’s Solution: Redesigning Our Streets for Pedestrians.

Fixing McGuinness and Beyond

Together with Mayor Mamdani, we are FINALLY FIXING MCGUINNESS. We can reorient our intersections for safety and invest in bike lanes and open streets, funding public transportation and limiting the reckless driving that we’ve been all too used to.
