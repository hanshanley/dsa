Transportation

As a representative of Cambridge and Somerville, Tim Toomey is committed to the cause of maintaining and improving our public and private transportation programs and infrastructure. Tim continues to fight to improve access to public transportation throughout our city, and is committed to ensuring that his neighbors reap the benefits of the transportation burdens that they already bear.

Tim’s Priorities

MBTA Green Line Extension

The extension of the Green Line is a crucial transportation project for our community. Unusually high rates of asthma in Somerville and Cambridge have been linked to the large volume of cars, trucks, buses, and trains that pass through our cities every day, making the construction of a clean and efficient public transportation system essential to our community’s health. Tim is committed to ensuring that the Green Line Extension becomes a reality. When it is complete, he wants the Green Line Extension to be something that we can all be proud of, and he will continue to make your voice heard as the project moves forward.

Under the leadership of Tim Toomey and the Somerville Legislative delegation, MassDOT and the MBTA have adopted the Construction Manager/General Contractor delivery process, which allowed a phased construction process to begin in 2012. Efforts to construct the extension are now well under way and Green Line service to Union Square is expected to begin in 2017. In the meantime, Tim has been working with Green Line abutters to make sure that their voices are heard during the property-acquisition and construction phases of this project.

Building the Grand Junction Rail Trail

Tim is working to make the Grand Junction railroad into a positive piece of infrastructure for our community. He has been a leader in the effort to construct a mixed-use path in the unused space adjacent to the tracks. Such a path would stretch from Somerville to the Charles River, connecting East Cambridge, Kendall Square, and Cambridgeport in the process. Tim will continue to work at both the local and state levels to make this project a part of future plans for the Grand Junction.

Grounding the McCarthy Overpass

Tim Toomey, like most members of the Somerville Community, believes that we need to remove the McCarthy Overpass that carries McGrath Highway over Washington St. and turn it into a more pedestrian- and cycle-friendly urban boulevard. Tim has been active in the long-term Grounding McGrath effort and has advocated for short-term improvements that will help improve pedestrian and bicycle safety on the streets surrounding the overpass.
