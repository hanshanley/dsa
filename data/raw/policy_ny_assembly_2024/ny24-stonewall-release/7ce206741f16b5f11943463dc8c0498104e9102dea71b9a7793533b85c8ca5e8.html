2024 Candidate Questionnaire Responses

April 21, 2024 by Stonewall Democrats

2024 will be a critical year for elections in our city, state, and country. SDNYC is committed to endorsing, campaigning for, and electing representatives who will fight to advance the rights, priorities, and ideals of the LGBTQ community.

Below are the candidates who submitted requests for our endorsement, which includes filling out our candidate questionnaires. Thank you to all the candidates who applied, and who took the time to engage with our club and our issues. We look forward to working with you to advance the rights of the LGBTQ community going forward.
