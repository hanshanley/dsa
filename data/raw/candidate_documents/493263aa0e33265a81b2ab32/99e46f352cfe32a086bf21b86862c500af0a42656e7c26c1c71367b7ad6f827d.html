State Representative, 7th District

KC Huffman

Democrat

Occupation: Attorney and high school baseball coach

Occupational Background: Attorney at Bromley Newton Huffman (current); Attorney at Thorp Purdy Jewett Urness & Wilkinson, P.C. (2001-2022); Judicial Clerk for the Hon. Lyle C. Velure (2000-2001)

Educational Background: I grew up and went to school in Reedsport, Oregon ('93). After high school I attended Linfield College, in McMinnville, Oregon where I earned a Bachelor of Science in Finance with a Minor in Political Science ('97). After Linfield, I attended the University of Oregon School of Law where I earned my Doctor of Jurisprudence ('00).

Prior Governmental Experience: I have not held elected office, but I do have experience representing elected officials and various government entities.

Hello, I'm KC, a husband, father of four, attorney, baseball coach, community volunteer, and Springfield resident for 23 years. I am asking for your vote to serve as the Democratic nominee for the Oregon House of Representatives for District 7.

What do I believe in?

  • The opportunity to succeed in school, in business, and in society.
  • That kindness is a key to being successful.
  • That we can, we must, and we will, create a better atmosphere in our State politics.

How will I make a difference?

  • As a husband and father of two girls and two boys, I've learned one size does not fit all, so we must remain open minded.
  • As an attorney, I've learned there are times to stand up and fight and there are times to make the best deal you can and then move on.
  • As a baseball coach, I've learned small details make big differences, so being prepared is a must.
  • As a community volunteer, I've learned we are each responsible for building the community we want, so we need to be willing to roll up our sleeves and get involved.
  • As a Springfield resident for 23 years, I've learned we are resilient, we are forward thinking, and we are proud.

I have not asked for endorsements.

Thank you.
