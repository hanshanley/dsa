Election Results

Home Election Results

Listed below are recent and archived election results. Many reports are offered in regular and condensed versions. Additional election data may be found at the State Board website.

Note on terminology –
Election results are accumulated by the candidate and can also be reported with the separate party lines.
A recanvass report will break down the vote totals for a candidate-contest by Election District and can also be reported with the separate party lines.
