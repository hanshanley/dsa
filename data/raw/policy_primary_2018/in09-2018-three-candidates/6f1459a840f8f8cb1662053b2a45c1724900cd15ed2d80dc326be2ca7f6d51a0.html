3 candidates face off in Democratic primary for Indiana's 9th Congressional District
Updated: 4:16 PM EDT May 1, 2018
Editorial Standards ⓘ
Mark Vanderhoff
Reporter

In southern Indiana, there's a race that Democrats are watching with hopes they can flip a congressional seat.

Dan Canon, Rob Chatlos and Liz Watson are running in the Democratic primary in Indiana's 9th District.

Advertisement

The candidate in this primary needs to convince voters he or she can take on the likely winner of the Republican primary, incumbent Trey Hollingsworth.

Hollingsworth has money and represents a largely conservative district.

"Thirty-one labor unions have endorsed me, and what that means is tens of thousands of voters in our district,” Watson said.

"I've been without health insurance, I've lived hand to mouth, I've done a lot of things that traditional politicians haven't done but that regular folks are doing on the ground in Indiana every day,” Canon said.

"I'm a truck driver. I'm working class. I think that gives me the opportunity to talk to people outside maybe what typical politicians focus their attention to,” Chatlos said.

Watson is an attorney from Bloomington and former labor policy advisor to Democrats in Congress.

Canon is a civil rights lawyer from southern Indiana who went before the U.S. Supreme Court in the landmark gay marriage case.

Chatlos is quick to point out he's not a lawyer.

All three agree addressing the opioid epidemic is a top priority.

"Find out what their financial funding requirements are and just work with my counterparts in Congress to provide adequate funding for our area,” Chatlos said.

"It involves access to harm reduction strategies. So needle sharing, access to naloxone and access to alternative pain medications like medical marijuana,” Canon said.

"We here in the United States have sky-high health care costs and yet we have worse outcomes than a lot of other industrialized countries. I believe that what we should do is go to a Medicare-for-all system,” Watson said.

Watson has edged out Canon in fundraising. She's raised $586,000 versus $434,000 by Canon.

Chatlos said he is not raising money and has not reported any contributions.
