We just launched our 2026 re-election campaign

January 28, 2026
  • Share on Facebook
  • Share on Twitter
Back to News

This past weekend, we launched our re-election campaign for Congress amidst Trump and ICE’s ongoing violence against our communities.

I need your support, not just to power our movement to stand up to Trump and to make our country better and more equitable for all, but also to prepare for our Democratic primary election.

We recently learned that two candidates have filed to run against us and we’ve seen outside groups polling residents in order to gauge whether they should get involved in the race.

We saw what happened in the 2024 election when right-wing AIPAC millionaires and billionaires flooded Democratic primaries, defeating two of our progressive allies. That’s why we’re preparing now for our re-election, because we need voices in Congress who are willing to take on the billionaire bullies, corporate greed, and the pro-genocide lobby.

I was sent to Congress to hold Trump accountable and fight for an economy that puts working people and vulnerable communities first. During his first term in office, we impeached Trump not once, but twice. 

In my time in Congress, I’ve introduced and passed legislation that invests in clean water, stopped water shutoffs, and invests directly in our communities, including in senior and community centers. In my district alone, we’ve invested $52 million:

✔ $23.6 Million for Community Spaces (parks, schools, health centers, green spaces, and more)

✔ $12.3 Million for Water Projects (lead pipe replacement, water lines/systems, pumps)

✔ $6.6 Million for Housing (affordable, home repairs, shelters)

✔ $5.5 Million for Economic Development (training, workforce, broadband, and more)

✔ $2 Million for Roads

✔ $1.5 Million for Public Services (fire department and equipment)

You can read more about my receipts in Congress and in my congressional district.

I am running for re-election because our work together in Congress is far from done. 

We’re demanding justice from Detroit to Gaza. We’re demanding ICE out of our communities and an end to forced family separation. We’re demanding an economy that puts everyday people first, not billionaires and greedy corporations. And, instead of more blank checks for war and military aggression, we’re demanding investments in our communities here at home.

If you can, please donate today to power our re-election campaign and fight for a future that includes all of us, not just the wealthy few.

Thank you for being by my side as, together, we demand a government that puts everyday people first.

Always serving you,

Rashida
