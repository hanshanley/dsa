2026 Candidate Questionnaire Responses

2026 Primaries
Key Dates and Candidate Responses

The Caribbean-American Political Action Committee (C-PAC) prepared and circulated questionnaires to regional candidates for the 2026 Primary Elections.

As you prepare to cast your ballot, please take a few minutes to review the responses C-PAC received. Click on the drop-downs below to read.

For ease of access, below are key 2026 Election Year dates for the Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia region.

  • Primary early voting begins: 8 June 2026
  • Primary early voting ends: 14 June 2026
  • Primary election day: 16 June 2026
  • General election early voting begins: 26 October 2026
  • General election early voting ends: 1 November 2026
  • Election Day: 3 November 2026
  • Primary deadline for voters to register affiliation and party registration: 2 June 2026
  • Primary early voting begins: 11 June 2026
  • Primary early voting ends: 18 June 2026
  • Primary election day: 23 June 2026
  • General election voter registration deadline: 13 October 2026
  • General election early voting begins: 22 October 2026
  • General election early voting ends: 29 October 2026
  • Election Day: 3 November 2026
  • Primary voter update and registration deadline: 5 June 2026
  • Primary date: 16 June 2026
  • Primary early in-person voting begins: 1 May 2026
  • Primary early in-person voting ends: 13 June 2026
  • General election voter updates and registration deadline: 23 October 2026
  • General election early, in-person voting begins: 18 September 2026
  • General election early, in-person voting ends: 31 October 2026
  • Election day: 3 November 2026
